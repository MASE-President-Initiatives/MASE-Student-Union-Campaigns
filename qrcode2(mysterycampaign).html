BEFORE YOU BEGIN

THE FOUR CLUES

This investigation contains four challenges. Each answer will reveal one number. Put the four numbers together to discover your secret combination.

01 — Work together.
You may solve this investigation alone or with a partner. If you are in a pair, discuss each clue and agree on your answers.

02 — Follow the clues.
Read each question carefully. You can request a hint if you need one.

03 — Find the combination.
Every correct answer gives you one digit. Keep the digits in the order you discover them.

04 — Decode the mystery.
When you finish, write down your secret code. Use the separate Decoder Sheet to work out what it means.

THE CHALLENGE:
The first individual or pair to present the correct original code AND its correctly decoded message to the campaign organiser wins a prize.

Keep your answers and decoded message secret. Do not post them publicly.
